How Active Listening Overpowers:  

Decoding the HALO

Noise Detection

The external microphones continuously pick up ambient sounds from the environment. These sounds are sent to the ANC processor for analysis.

Signal Processing

The ANC processor analyses the incoming noise signals and creates an anti-noise signal. The anti-noise signal is essentially an inverted phase version of the ambient noise.

Sound Wave Interference

The anti-noise signal is played through the earbud speakers along with the desired audio content. When the anti-noise signal meets the ambient noise inside the ear, the two sound waves (which are 180 degrees out of phase) interfere with each other.

Noise Reduction 

This interference effectively cancels out the unwanted ambient noise, reducing the overall noise level that reaches the listener's ear.
